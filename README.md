AI-Commodity-Radar is a one-page dashboard for Indian gold and silver buyers. It shows near-live gold and silver prices in ₹ and $, tracks how the rupee is moving against the dollar, pulls in the world news that moves precious metals, and uses AI to give a simple Buy / Wait / Sell read with reasons and risks.

Features
- Gold (24K/22K per 10 g) and silver (per kg) prices that refresh every 15 seconds
- Rupee vs dollar strength with 7-day, 30-day and 3-month changes
- 3-month price charts with RSI and moving-average indicators
- Filterable news from NewsData.io and Google News
- AI advisor combining price indicators and headlines, with a rule-based fallback if the AI is unavailable
- Dark, responsive, animated UI; works on phones and as plain static files

Stack: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Google Gemini API, NewsData.io, gold-api.com.

Not financial advice. Indian prices are estimates (international price + import duty + GST) and exclude jeweller making charges.
